01 — Brand idea

Connection is the product

Dabarli is Tunisia’s flexible work and gig marketplace. The symbol shows two stylized figures overlapping at the shoulder — not a crowd, not a corporation, but a handshake made visible.

The arch of negative space between them is intentional: room for work to pass through. Protect that void the way you protect the brand name.

  • Green is the only brand hue — light and dark modes share one palette
  • Blue is retired from identity (legacy assets must be replaced)
  • Arabic wordmark دبرلي sits under the mark on splash, or beside it in product headers

05 — Typography

Geometric, friendly, clear

Product UI uses the app type stack. This guide uses Syne for display titles, Manrope for Latin body and the Latin wordmark, and Cairo for Arabic display.

  • Wordmark casing is sentence case: Dabarli — never all-caps
  • Keep Latin and Arabic wordmarks visually balanced; don’t stretch either
  • Arabic letter-spacing stays at 0 — never track out دبرلي
  • UI weights 400–700; headlines may use 800

06 — Color

Green structure, mist space, night rest

Blue is out of the identity. Leaf carries attention; Mist and Night carry surfaces. Ratio target: ~55% Mist/Night surfaces · 35% Ink text · 10% Leaf accents.

Pairings: Leaf Deep for text & CTAs on Mist/Paper · Leaf for marks on Night · white wordmark on Night · never use Leaf as small UI text on Mist (contrast is too weak).
